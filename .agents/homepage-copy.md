## 3. PROBLEM: "The leak you can't see"

**Section header:** Your phone rang last night. Nobody heard it.

Appointment-based businesses miss 23 to 42 percent of their inbound calls. Not because the team is lazy. Because calls arrive at lunch, at 9pm, on Sunday, and three at a time.

And here is the part that hurts: 78 percent of after-hours callers never leave a voicemail. They do not call back. They scroll to the next name on Google and book there.

The same leak runs through every channel:

- The WhatsApp message that sat unread until morning, by which time she booked elsewhere
- The web form inquiry that got a reply eight hours later, to a lead that went cold in five minutes
- The no-show nobody called back, so the slot earned nothing
- The happy customer nobody asked for a review, so your competitor's rating grew instead

Each one looks small. Together they are the most expensive line item in your business, and it appears on no report.

**Transition:** You cannot hire your way out of a 168-hour week. You can capture it.

## Build notes for Claude Code

1. No em dashes anywhere in rendered copy. This file uses none; keep it that way in JSX.
2. No fabricated numbers, counters, ticker animations, or invented testimonials. Only the proof points listed here.
3. Benchmark stats (23 to 42 percent, 78 percent) must read as industry benchmarks, not Awaaz results.
4. "AI" appears in body copy as the mechanism. Never in the H1 as the value.
5. One primary CTA repeated down the page: the free leak audit. Do not compete with it.
6. Schema: Organization + Service + FAQPage JSON-LD on this page (ai-seo skill: FAQ blocks are the most extractable format for AI answer engines; keep answers under 50 words).
7. Every claim above the fold must be true today. If a channel (e.g. email automation) is not live yet, move it below the fold or cut it.
